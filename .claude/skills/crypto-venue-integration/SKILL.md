# Crypto Venue Integration

Ground every venue/fee/capacity claim against the fetch code and fee tables below. Two conclusions are load-bearing and closed: **funding arb is signal-only (it does not beat risk-free)**, and **cash & carry is not validated until a real settlement lands**. Do not present either lane as a live executable edge.

## Two proven-negative backtest conclusions — both closed
**(1) Funding arb does NOT beat risk-free — signal only, never auto-execution.** The repo encodes funding's best case as **2.79%/yr, below the 4.0% risk-free benchmark**: `scripts/basis-hold-to-expiry.js:62-63` (`RISK_FREE_PCT = 4.0`, `FUNDING_BEST_PCT = 2.79`), compared at `:411-412` (`BEATS`/`BELOW`). The mechanism: the lane "is killed by the 0.10% x2 SPOT TAKER fee, not by lack of persistence" (`scripts/funding-targeted-edge.js:456`); "If it cannot beat risk-free at these fees, it will not beat risk-free at Diego's" (`scripts/funding-maker-entry-taker-hedge.js:45-46`). **Precision note:** the phrase "fee-holiday artifact" is **not** in the codebase — do not quote it as if it were. The real, citable artifacts are (a) the 2.79%-below-4.0% constants above, (b) the re-entry **cadence artifact** (`scripts/funding-backtest.js:363`; `funding-targeted-edge.js:336,345`), and (c) the theoretical zero-fee ceiling that "nothing can beat" (`scripts/funding-fee-optimization.js:275-277`). The code also explicitly refuses to assume Binance's recurring zero-fee USDC promos (`lib/funding-math.js:104-112`). Funding is a signal surface only.

**(2) Cash & carry is provisional — not validated until a real settlement lands.** The code labels any unexpired locked position `LOCKED_NOT_REALIZED` ("carries settlement and venue risk"), and marks a contract `REALIZED` "only for contracts whose expiry has PASSED and whose settlement we sourced" (`scripts/basis-hold-to-expiry.js:27-33,224-226`); settlement price is "a VERIFIER, not a P&L input" (`:310-312`). **Precision note:** there is **no `2026-07-31` (or "provisional"/"pending settlement") string in the code** — validity is keyed per-contract-expiry, not to a fixed calendar date. The product decision (Diego): do not treat the cash & carry backtest as validated until the first real settlement lands (his stated horizon for that is after 2026-07-31) — that date is a product statement, not a code citation. Until a `REALIZED` settlement exists, present cash & carry as provisional. (A stale May-2026 4.2–5.3% APY narrative in `PROJECT.md:244-266` is superseded by these honest script conclusions — do not cite it.)

**Do NOT re-run either backtest.** Both are explicitly closed to avoid overfitting, per product decision. Rerunning to "get a better number" is not authorized work.

## Capacity — real book-walk depth, min of both legs, NEVER open interest
Every capacity number traces to a walked order-book bid ladder; OI is never capacity even where it's fetched.
- Cross-venue perp-spot: `row.wholeTradeCapacityUsd = Math.min(spotCap, perpCap)` with `capacityBind` naming the binding leg (`agent28-perp-spot.js:184-186`); perp short leg is `walkPerpBidDepthUsd` within 20bps, "real book, NEVER OI" (`:93,15,106-119`).
- Dated carry: "Never infer depth from volume or open interest — OI is not size you can fill" (`agent19-basis.js:504`); rows pass `oiUsd: null` and `capacityUsdOverride: capacityUsd // REAL order-book depth (book-walk)` (`:878,986,1097,1205`). Min-of-legs in `lib/carry-optimize.js`: `capacityUsd = Math.min(futureCapUsd, spotCapUsd)` (`:225-231`), "Capacity must trace to a walked ladder. Never OI, never a proxy" (`:349,472`). Walk stays inside the slip band (`walkFutureBids` `:1024`, `ladderDepthUsd` `:342`, `SLIP_TOL` `:54`).

When adding a venue or capacity path: book-walk both legs, take `Math.min`, label the binding leg, and pass `oiUsd: null`. Never substitute OI or volume for depth. See [[nextjs-data-architecture]] for how these agent outputs reach the API, and [[pm2-fleet-ops]] for the writer→file→route chains.
